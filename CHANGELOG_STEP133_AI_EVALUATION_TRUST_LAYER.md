# STEP133 — VANDIRA AI Evaluation & Trust Layer

## Objective
Add a deterministic, transparent structural trust check between the AI answer/evidence layer and downstream decision surfaces.

## Changes
- Added a `TRUST CHECK` panel to assistant answers.
- Checks presence of intent route, constituency context, key signals, evidence availability appropriate to route freshness requirements, and returned answer text.
- Shows Strong / Review needed / Limited states.
- Explicitly labels the module as a structural grounding check, not an independent fact-check.
- Existing Evidence, Intelligence Brief, Decision Snapshot, Decision Workspace and Recommended Action layers remain intact.
- No election data, canonical calculations, or source records were modified.

## Guardrails
- No new political/election facts are generated by this layer.
- Missing evidence or data gaps lower trust status rather than being silently filled.
- Fresh-source routes require evidence availability to receive a fully strong structural status.
